# Research basis and falsifiable scope

Primary-source check: 2026-11-23. This note motivates a small certified-growth experiment; it is not a novelty or literature-completeness claim. The implemented theorem, assumptions and measured results must be read separately in the project proof and experiment records. The proposed contribution is the integration of an autoregressive model, a constrained decision readout, certificate-preserving growth and executed-path checks. None of those ingredients alone is new.

## What the primary sources establish

**Selective SSM stability.** [Cao et al., ICML 2026, *A Control-Theoretic View of Mamba on Stability and Robustness*](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/cao26l.html) explicitly distinguishes input-dependent/selective dynamics from a single stable LTI recurrence. The publisher abstract reports BIBO stability from continuous-time sampling, two-term robustness bounds growing linearly with sequence length, and common-quadratic-Lyapunov conditions for general LPV systems. These are layer stability/robustness results, not a language-model semantic safety theorem. Only the publisher abstract was accessible in this review: the linked PDF failed the web reader's content-type handling. We therefore do not import an uninspected theorem, constants, or implementation assumptions.

**A more recent accessible selective-SSM analysis.** [Zubić and Scaramuzza, arXiv:2505.11602v3, revised July 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.11602v3) separates selection from driving input. Theorem 7.1 derives ISS when a uniformly bounded positive-definite storage has a uniform decay inequality over every admissible selection and the input operator is bounded. Sections 8.4 and 8.6 sharply limit the empirical claim: a soft, sampled LMI penalty improves core diagnostics but is not a certified passivity condition, and it does not improve robust forecasting MSE in that study. This motivates a construction that remains feasible after every permitted update and covers the readout, rather than treating a regularization loss as a certificate.

**Trainability inside a certified family.** [Revay, Wang and Manchester, *Recurrent Equilibrium Networks*, arXiv:2104.05942v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2104.05942v3) provides contracting/robust recurrent families with direct unconstrained parameterizations. Theorem 1 assumes a slope-restricted activation and positive-definite matrix conditions to obtain well-posedness, contraction and specified incremental IQCs. This is precedent for effective weights obtained from free optimizer parameters. It does not establish the meaning of a generated decision, certify arbitrary optimizer arithmetic, or give this project's growth theorem. A simpler diagonal selective recurrence is preferable for the pilot because its reachable set and arithmetic assumptions can be audited directly; this is a tractability choice, not an expressivity superiority claim.

**Invariance is the relevant control concept.** [Xu, Tabuada, Grizzle and Ames, *Robustness of Control Barrier Functions for Safety Critical Control*, arXiv:1612.01554](https://arxiv.org/html/1612.01554) relates zeroing barrier conditions to forward invariance and studies disturbance-dependent relaxations. The useful lesson is to state a safe set, admissible disturbances and a condition that keeps trajectories inside it. Continuous-time barrier theorems do not automatically apply to a token recurrence, floating-point kernels, or a semantic interpretation of output. The project should prove its own discrete one-step implication and its readout consequence.

**Function preservation is established prior art.** [Chen, Goodfellow and Shlens, *Net2Net*, arXiv:1511.05641v4](https://arxiv.org/html/1511.05641v4) constructs wider/deeper networks representing the teacher function at insertion. Identity-layer insertion depends on the activation/domain; it is not available for every activation. Function preservation at insertion does not imply safety after later updates or lower cumulative training cost. For this project, growth must preserve both the effective model family and all vocabulary logits. Executable equality additionally requires preserving the old operation order and kernel path; equal real functions alone are insufficient.

**An external semantic baseline exists.** [Maghera et al., *AGDecode*, arXiv:2610.36105v1, October 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36105v1) is a real paper. It compiles semantic attribute-grammar specifications into relational constraints for external SMT/CHC-style oracles and claims soundness/completeness relative to an ideal oracle. Its C-program examples include path-sensitive safety properties. This supplies a relevant comparison for specification expressiveness and generation-time intervention, not evidence of intrinsic safety in a trained recurrence. A simple checker in this project is not an AGDecode reproduction and must not be labeled one.

**Floating-point verification is a separate obligation.** [Murray, *Lipschitz-Based Robustness Certification Under Floating-Point Execution*, arXiv:2603.13334v7, November 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.13334v7) gives counterexamples to real-arithmetic certification and composes explicit execution-deviation bounds with no-overflow conditions. Its activation assumptions include floating-point-exact operations; smooth sigmoid/tanh are excluded from that direct result. It cannot simply certify this model. Independently, [PyTorch's numerical-accuracy documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/stable/notes/numerical_accuracy.html) warns that mathematically identical batched/sliced computations, platforms and releases need not be bitwise identical. Tests must pin actual execution settings and state what remains assumed about elementary functions and backend arithmetic.

## Construction choices and counterexamples

The following are this project's deductions, not claims borrowed from the cited papers.

Individually stable full matrices are insufficient under switching. For example,

```text
A1 = [[1/2, 2], [0, 1/2]]
A2 = [[1/2, 0], [2, 1/2]]
A2 A1 = [[1/4, 1], [1, 17/4]].
```

Each matrix has spectral radius 1/2. Their symmetric positive product has largest eigenvalue greater than 17/4, so repeated alternation is unbounded. Checking only eigenvalues of each input-conditioned transition would miss this failure.

For a componentwise recurrence `h' = a * h + (1-a) * v`, `h,v` in the unit cube and `a` in `[0,1]` imply cube invariance in real arithmetic. With `a <= rho < 1`, two states driven by the *same* selector and injection contract in the infinity norm. This does not by itself give a bound for two different input sequences: selector and injection sensitivity must also be bounded. Added arithmetic disturbance requires either a larger invariant set, inward slack, or an explicitly bounded horizon. An unconstrained bias, residual bypass, normalization or readout can invalidate the end-to-end argument even if this recurrence remains bounded.

The decision property should be stated as a strict raw-logit margin: on every reachable admissible state whose authenticated evidence fails the declared support criterion, `logit(DEFER) > logit(CONFIRM)`. This implies greedy argmax cannot emit that exact CONFIRM token at a covered decision position, regardless of other vocabulary logits. It does not imply greedy emission of DEFER, a grammatical completion, or refusal expressed in arbitrary prose. Sampling at positive temperature can still emit CONFIRM if its logit is finite. A theorem for greedy selection must not be reported as a sampling theorem.

## Where the semantic bridge currently stops

Two externally authenticated evidence coordinates and a supplied two-evidence support criterion can define a meaningful conditional claim: if a coordinate says required evidence is absent, a modeled confirmation must not be emitted. The trusted interface must state the exact meaning, provenance, plan identity, lifetime and uncertainty of these coordinates. Text saying “evidence present” must not silently authenticate itself. During a generated continuation, a trusted coordinate cannot become valid merely because the model emits an evidence-looking token.

This first task has a substantial simplification: it supplies both the relevant state abstraction and the support criterion. The theorem then protects a fixed decision boundary; it does not prove the model learned to identify evidence, resolve references, bind evidence to the correct plan, or derive a criterion from prose. Calling this a proof of natural-language understanding would be false. Non-vacuity requires supported cases on which CONFIRM is permitted and actually selected, together with useful multi-token task performance. An always-defer trajectory can satisfy the exclusion theorem while failing the experiment.

The architecture may make this narrow exclusion intrinsic to its own raw forward pass, conditional on the trusted input contract. Authentication remains an external assumption. A downstream checker is an additional mechanism whose interventions must be reported separately from raw behavior.

## Prespecified, falsifiable next experiment

Use a small synthetic domain of candidate plans, resources and uncertain observations. Supply authenticated *primitive facts*, not a ready-made validity bit. For instance, two plans use overlapping resources; interval-valued observations, explicit identity tags and a compositional rule determine which plan is supported. Prompts request a short derivation, a plan identifier and a final decision. Keep actual uncertainty semantics exact in the generator, and freeze held-out combinations, renamed entities, distractors and longer chains before training.

The next theorem should connect a conservative reachable evidence state to those primitive facts. One possible direction is a separately specified sound interval/set transformer embedded in the recurrent state, with trainable bounded features supplying useful rankings inside its admissible region. This is an architectural research proposal, not an implemented result. If an external transformer does all inference, report that delegation explicitly; do not credit the learned SSM with the proof.

Falsification criteria: unsupported raw confirmation violates the claimed contract; no useful supported confirmations fails non-vacuity; explanations that contradict primitive facts fail task quality even when the final token is safe; improvement that disappears against same-size continuation/LR restart or matched larger scratch fails the growth-utility hypothesis. Report loss, useful completion accuracy, supported-case unnecessary deferral, exact raw continuations and cumulative compute separately.

For zero-connected added branches, an immediate learning prediction is available without invoking singular learning theory: if the new output coupling is zero, the new branch's internal gradient through that coupling is initially zero, while the coupling can receive a nonzero gradient from nonzero branch features. Making both features and coupling identically zero can leave the branch dormant. Measure coupling gradients and later internal parameter movement; parameter count alone is no evidence of added learnable capacity. No SLT-derived plateau trigger or benefit claim is used here. Growth timing must follow a predeclared development criterion; a short noisy pilot cannot establish a plateau.
