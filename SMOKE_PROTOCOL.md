# Ordinary-text pretraining smoke experiment

Train two byte-level autoregressive state-space language models from random initialization on privately mounted, manifest-pinned literary prose. The next-token cross entropy on ordinary documents is the whole training objective. Synthetic examples and the held-out evaluation questions have zero weight and zero training tokens. Only positive, verified rights information and locally approved documents are eligible. Do not concatenate documents across split boundaries. Report bytes or byte targets precisely, including framing if used.

## Before reading the measurements

- Seed 12, one block of width 16, 128 input positions, eight sequences per update, 128 nominal updates. Same byte vocabulary, initial tensor values, samples, Adam policy and held-out evaluation positions in both arms. The certified arm uses its controlled transition and signed decision readout throughout; the baseline relaxes both. This joint ablation cannot assign a difference in language quality to either change alone.
- Compare a 64-update checkpoint continued for another 64 updates with a separate uninterrupted 128-update run at the same seed. Compare fitted tensors, optimizer contents, draws and held-out scores. A serialized file hash alone is insufficient.
- Hold out works by different authors for validation and final evaluation. Report initial and final held-out next-byte loss (natural log units), targets and perplexity with the same denominator. Training exposure beyond the available unique bytes is repeated reading.
- Instrument the actual update path. Verify finite parameters, the allowed effective representation, the trained hidden-state box and the protected decision ordering before/after updates and at insertion of a zero-connected block. Record the comparator's observed behavior. A finite sample of hidden states complements the family-level proof.
- Preserve raw model generation using prefill and one-token decoding. Use an isolated fictional evaluation to distinguish correctness of the reserved action channel from ordinary-language assent, refusal, uncertainty and unclassifiable text. Score affirmative and negative cases separately. Keep fixture contents, reference answers and model outputs private.
- Growth is an insertion check on a disposable copy of a fitted model. Verify the full vocabulary logits at every position for the same path and dimensions before and after addition, including the state-family checks. No trained growth or plateau claim follows from this smoke.
- Preserve all existing Omen action-time limits and one numerical worker. This branch may release source, tests and sanitized aggregate evidence only. The personal corpus, document texts, checkpoints, machine-specific paths, per-example answers and local evaluation records stay outside the branch.

The primary deliverable is evidence that constraints are active during genuine prose training and what they cost in this measured setting. A decrease in cross entropy cannot establish semantic alignment. Generalization from a single short literary training source, one random seed or these few downstream cases would require a new study.
